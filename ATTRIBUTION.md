### Three.js
- **Author**: Ricardo Cabello (mrdoob) and Three.js authors
- **Version**: r128 (UMD build)
- **Source**: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
- **License**: MIT (`/vendor/THREE_LICENSE`)
